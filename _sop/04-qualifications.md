## 4.1 General

- Qualifications are issued by the approving authority for the matter concerned. A team of Controller quals ordinarily vets Controller exams, hosts vet hosting exams, and so on for other classes. Command holds final say.
- To obtain a qualification a candidate shall pass the exam where one exists.
- No candidate shall pass an exam by exemption. Prior experience and recommendation may shorten preparation only.

## 4.2 Training Structure

A qualification path comprises one or more of the following stages. Not all stages apply to every qualification.

| Stage | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Class | Reading or briefing material supporting the related qualification. Where a qualification is class-only it is for personal knowledge and attendance is not required to function in an operation. May be delivered as linked material, a group session, or a 1:1 sit-down. |
| Exam | Paired with a class where the qualification affords additional quality of life or lifts a limitation. Unproctored; the candidate is trusted not to cheat. |
| Assessment | Proctored, and ordinarily requires presence in an official capacity to enact the qualification. The strictest route, and the usual path to staff qualifications. |
| Qualification | Issued on completion of the required stages. |

## 4.3 For All Personnel

- Road Crew Class. Covers general information about our operations, the required sections of this SOP, and operating tips. The qualification received is the Member role, replacing Newcomer.
- Steamer Class. Covers effective use of steam locomotives in the valley and includes an exam. The qualification received is Steam Cert.

## 4.4 For Staff

- Controller Qual. Controllers are considered low-level staff. Requires an exam and an assessment; a class is available and optional.
- Hosting Qual. Hosts are low to medium-level staff. Requires an exam and an assessment; the class is mandatory.

## 4.5 Removal or Suspension

Qualifications may be suspended or withdrawn by administrative authority where necessary for performance, conduct, inactivity, or other operational concern.
