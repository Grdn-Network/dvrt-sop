## 1.1 Purpose and Applicability

This Standard Operating Procedure provides the single clean reference for the conduct of all official GRDN OPS : DVMP sessions. Guidelines herein shall be complied with in full and shall not be excluded during SOP-compliant operations; Procedures apply on activation by the controlling authority.

- All members are encouraged to familiarise themselves with this document. Staff shall be held to it in full.
- This is a living document and may see frequent revision. Crews shall refer to the current published version and to Command for authoritative guidance.
- Critical information is additionally embedded in the Discord and linked from the rules; crews may acquire much of it through play alone.

Guardian Operations, abbreviated GRDN OPS, is a community organised around operational gameplay. Enjoyment is supported; the core values are Semi-Realism, Respect, and Excellence.

## 1.3 Server Rules

- The rules of the GRDN OPS server are published under the Info category and apply at all times.
- Rules 7 and 10 apply to operations specifically. Violation shall affect a member’s standing and experience within Operations.

## 1.4 Roster Progression

- New joiners are carried on the roster under the Newcomer role.
- On full attendance of one official operation the host shall convert the member to the Member role, denoting demonstrated familiarity with our operations.

## 1.5 Classification of Operations

| Class | Oversight | Assurance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official | Vetted host, CMD oversight | Run IAW this SOP. Reasonable performance expected, bot tooling available, feedback routed to Command for improvement. |
| Unofficial | None | May be hosted by anybody. No access to bot tools or official resources. No guarantee of modpack, procedure, or conduct is made by CMD. |

The Official designation denotes assurance of standard, not preference. No member is required to operate official sessions, and unofficial play shall not be held to be incorrect.

## 1.6 Joining an Operation

- Operations run to a published schedule. Consult the Events tab in the Discord.
- On entry, select a locomotive for which you are licensed IAW 2.4.
- Set your locomotive number using GRDNConnect.
- Proceed IAW Part 2 - Crews and Part 3 - Mandatory Procedures.
- Questions are welcome and shall not be discouraged. Further project information is published at www.grdnnetwork.com.

## 1.7 GRDN Bot

Run /setcrew in Discord to allocate your role, train number, and preferred name for the session.

## 1.8 Approved Mod Suite

| Modification | Operational Function |
| --- | --- |
| DLE (Derail Logistics Engine) | Consist creation and job assignment backend used by Logistics. |
| Remote Dispatch | Territory view and switch control, available to all crews. The fork in use is specified per operation. |
| Double Track | Permits opposing and overtaking movements. |
| Signals | Provides the signal aspects governing controlled territory. |
| GRDNConnect | In-game train numbering, voice channel movement, and host functions. |
| CommsAPI | Required backend for GRDNConnect. |
| Map Image Changer | Renders the valley map in game. |

## 1.9 GRDNConnect - First-Time Linkage

- Load into the session.
- Operate the cab radio (GRDN Crew or GRDN Radio).
- A linkage prompt shall appear in #ops-chat.
- Confirm the prompt to bind your in-game reported Steam ID to your Discord ID.

Thereafter the Comms radio permits movement between Discord channels without leaving the game. This capability is required for control handovers IAW 3.6.
