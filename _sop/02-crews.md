## 2.1 Road Crew

- The standard crew type. Carries no insignia by default.
- Road Crew are responsible for the movement of freight, passengers, or empty equipment across the valley.
- Road Crew shall normally operate under the authority of Dispatch.
- Road Crew may be further subdivided by the procedure in force - for example into Locals and Interchange crews when a hub-to-hub system is run.

## 2.2 Yard Crew (Shunters)

- The secondary crew type, ordinarily denoted by the suffix #S.
- Yard Crew are responsible for the assembly, breaking, and organisation of consists within a nominated yard, under a Logistics Officer or Yardmaster.
- Yard Crew are afforded procedural advantages and are ordinarily not held to strict callout formats, including callsigns, when working directly with a Yardmaster.
- Where no Yardmaster or qualified Controller is available, Yard Crew may own their station as Controller for the duration.

## 2.3 Brakeman

- A Brakeman does not operate a locomotive; the position works in tandem with a train driver or shunter.
- Brakemen are principally employed during shunting, riding the rear of a consist to pass distance information to the engineer and to couple or uncouple equipment.
  - Distance calls shall be acknowledged by the engineer.
- In the case of repeated or intentional poor conduct as engine crew, a member shall be demoted to the Brakeman role.
  - Members holding this role are permitted to serve as Brakeman only, not as Road or Yard Crew.
  - Restoration depends on circumstance. A member shall ordinarily be expected to work several operations without incident and in good sport.

## 2.4 Locomotive Licensing

> **NOTICE** Framework only - published for future implementation. Licensing gates are not yet enforced across all operations.
{: .danger}

- Operations may invoke locomotive licensing for crews.
- DE6, S282, S060, and DH4 are given priority to existing members.
- Members may request attendance of a Steam class where required, and may request additional training on other classes.

## 2.5 Demonstrators

- Demonstrators are awarded to crews who show exceptional usage of their train - including but not limited to efficiency, extremely heavy hauls, speed, and handling.
- Owners are given their train to mark the achievement and to represent the company.
- Owners may pawn a demonstrator out to others but retain driving priority.
- Frequent Controllers may hold a demonstrator but shall share it while controlling.
- Owners are expected to remain active.
