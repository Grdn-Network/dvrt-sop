## 5.1 Basics

- Controllers are low-level staff who take operational control during an operation. Their focus is moving trains toward their objectives and destinations.
- Controllers shall work together where required to accomplish that mission, and are strongly advised to communicate in a defined, efficient manner to prevent confusion and incident.
- Controllers requiring a framework are advised to adopt common defined procedures IAW Part 8.
- A Controller may wear all, some, or none of the roles below. An operation may not field a Controller of every type.
  - For example, a Controller may claim Dispatch clearance over East but not West, and may additionally hold Logistics Officer for Harbour but not for all stations.
- Controllers may submit incident reports via Op-Records.

## 5.2 Dispatching

- Dispatchers are responsible for the movement and clearance of Road Crew on the main line between stations.
- Dispatchers shall aim to route consists by the most efficient route at the highest practical speed with minimal delay, accounting for consist weight, estimated top speed, weather conditions, and traffic en route.
- In higher-traffic operations Dispatch may be split East and West of the central river, or by any other defined split.
- The Remote Dispatch mod is used to view consists and set switches across the valley.

## 5.3 Hub Control / Yardmasters

- Yard Controllers are responsible for all movements, clearances, and signals within the yard or set of yards they control.
- A Controller shall not clear traffic onto, or set switches on, main track without the permission of the relevant Dispatcher or a defined procedure. This shall ideally be conducted through transfer procedures IAW 8.4.
- A Controller shall act to create the least wasted time for inbound and outbound Road Crew, to include:
  - Ensuring shunters do not obstruct the main when traffic is inbound.
  - Having outbound consists ready for departure at all times.
  - Coordinating with the Logistics Officer to allocate freight to crews.
- Hub Control is ordinarily responsible for directing one or more yard crews in the conduct of these operations.

## 5.4 Logistics

- Logistics Officers are responsible for creating and assigning consists using the Derail Logistics Engine, or acting as a valley-wide Yardmaster assigning locomotives and jobs without DLE.
- Logistics decides which crew runs to which station with which load.
