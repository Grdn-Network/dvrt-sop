## 3.1 Communication Standard

- Transmissions shall be brief, relevant, and unambiguous. State who you are calling first, then who you are.
- Once a clear back-and-forth is established, callsigns need not be restated on every transmission unless restatement prevents confusion. A break in traffic resets this allowance.
- Critical Information shall be repeated back and confirmed where required.
- Extended discussion, troubleshooting, or training shall be moved to a BCKP channel where practical.
  - Crews in a BCKP channel remain cleared to their stated limit and shall check back in with the Controller for further clearance before proceeding beyond it.
- Crews operating at or above tonnage capacity may append “Heavy” to their callsign, which grants the priorities and exceptions attaching to that status.
- Callsigns may differ between Controllers, yard crews, and demonstrators. The correct callsign shall be used where known.
- A Controller may adopt a callsign representing their scope - for example Logistics - and that callsign shall be used in preference to the general “Dispatch”.
- On first contact a request call may be made to establish communication before passing further information.

## 3.2 Call Formats

First contact: [who you are calling], [who you are] [train details], [location]. Request: [who you are calling], [who you are], request? Standard traffic: [who you are calling], [who you are], [location / job / track], [request or intent].

> **First contact - examples**
>
> "Harbour, 023 S060, passing Foxtrot 7 wye, inbound to Echo Nine."
> "Hub Control, 064 DE6 heavy, approaching from the north with a 500 m consist."
{: .example}

> **Standard traffic - examples**
>
> "Harbour, 023, Foxtrot 7 wye, requesting fuel service."
> "Harbour, 047, holding short military yard, with you looking for E9I."
> "Dispatch, 063, with you east of Farm, for FF."
{: .example}

## 3.3 Communication Failsafe

Where a crew is unclear which authority controls them, what limit applies, or whether authority to continue exists, the crew shall slow the locomotive and request clarification before proceeding.

## 3.4 Control Authority - Dark Territory

Dark Territory is any location not under the control of an active Dispatcher.

- Crews shall monitor their surroundings continuously to avoid collision, using Ops Radio (East/West) to coordinate with nearby Road Crew.
- A train arriving at an occupied station shall establish contact and develop a solution. Yard Crew may issue clearances outside active dispatch scope to facilitate coordinated station movements.
- Signals shall be treated as early warning of conflict. Any unexpected aspect or unexpected change shall be reported immediately.
- Movements in Dark Territory or on other than main track are responsible for setting their own switches and verifying routing.
- Movements with no train in the vicinity require no communication or request.

## 3.5 Control Authority - Controlled Territory

- Movement is governed by the Controller holding the area of operation.
- Switches may or may not be lined. The Controller shall clarify on issue of the movement order; where unclear, the crew shall assume switches are not lined and keep active watch.
- Where switch control within an area is delegated to a shunting movement, that movement becomes responsible for those switches.
- Signals shall be obeyed at all times except where explicit permission is given by the Controller.
- A crew approaching a red signal believed to be set incorrectly may query the Controller but shall not break aspect before clearance is granted.

> **Signal exception - examples**
>
> "086, Hub Control, you are clear to proceed through the next red signal."
> "077, Harbour Control, you are clear to violate signals on the east side of Echo yard."
> "038, Control, break signal aspect and follow company ahead with caution."
{: .example}

## 3.6 Control Transfer

- On approach to the limit of the Controller’s zone the crew shall be instructed to transfer. Crews shall not change voice channels before being cleared to do so.
- The crew shall switch to the receiving control zone using the Comms radio and make a clear first contact call to the receiving Controller.
  - Where the channel is occupied with operational traffic, wait your turn.
  - Where the channel is occupied with non-operational traffic, make an assertive request call and await response.
- Where the transfer is into Dark Territory, the crew shall announce themselves and their intentions.
- No crew shall leave a control zone until clearance is explicitly granted.
