## 7.1 Basics

- This Part carries information shared across all operations that is not detailed elsewhere. Operation-specific detail is held in the subordinate sections.
- Procedures applying to a specific operation are found in Part 8 - Protocols and Procedures.
- An operation may enforce stricter requirements than those published here but shall not lift restrictions.

## 7.2 Official Operations

Official operations are held to the standard set in 1.5 and run per SOP. This document is followed substantially one-to-one; deviations shall be declared by the host.

## 7.3 Company Realistic

Company Realistic, formerly US OPS, is our flagship operation style, focused on semi-serious gameplay, communications, and presentation. Created and led by our founder, Guardian.

## 7.4 Continuously Better Transport

Continuously Better Transport, abbreviated CBT, is an extra-hardcore operation style in which the road to success is nowhere to be seen and mistakes are severely punished. Created and led by our host, Arnoldio.

| Area | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Comms | Required at all times. |
| Main / Dark / Yard | Mainline is always covered by a Dispatcher. Destination stations are assigned to the first Road Crew to arrive and revoked on departure unless otherwise specified. A station not in use is considered mainline and is off limits. |
| Usernames | In-game usernames are kept as-is. |
| Locomotive rating | All locomotives are free to use. Passing a trial reserves a specified locomotive number for your use per the leaderboard; you may use all locomotives up to and including the one earned. An obtained licence does not guarantee a free locomotive where all of that type are in use. |
| Trials | One unified run on a predetermined route and yard against a reference time and tonnage, completed within the allowed deviation. |
| Server restart | Every 150 - 180 minutes. |
